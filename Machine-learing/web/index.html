💡 Why Random Forest Wins

With 98.35% precision, Random Forest produced only 2 false positive alerts across 859 retained subscribers, preventing unnecessary promotional discounts from being awarded to safe customers.

⚡ High Recall Alternative

Gradient Boosting captured 121 out of 141 churners (85.82% recall). In business scenarios where the loss of a client is catastrophic, this model offers the strongest protective barrier.

📉 Why Logistic Regression Struggles

Complex non-linear interactions (e.g. high customer service calls combined with daytime usage spikes) cannot be separated linearly, causing 180 false alarms.
